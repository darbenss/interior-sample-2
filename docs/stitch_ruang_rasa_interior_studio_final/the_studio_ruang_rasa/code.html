THE ATELIER / JAKARTA

Origins

Founded in the heart of Indonesia, Ruang Rasa emerged from a desire to reconcile the rich, tactile heritage of Southeast Asian craftsmanship with the rigorous geometries of contemporary minimalism. We believe that true luxury is found in restraint, in the spaces left intentionally blank.

Our practice is an ongoing dialogue between raw materials and refined form. Every space we conceive is a study in light, shadow, and silence—a deliberate retreat from the noise of the modern world, prioritizing timeless functionality over fleeting trends.

PRINCIPAL

Elara Lin

LEAD ARCHITECT & FOUNDER

With a background spanning classical architecture and industrial design, Elara approaches interiors not as decoration, but as volumetric composition. Her work is characterized by a profound respect for materiality and an unwavering commitment to spatial harmony.
